PRECISE WORKFLOW
1. Select a design and click its Change 1st colour / 2nd colour button.
2. For close shades, click Pick exact part. The editor displays the ORIGINAL.
3. Click the precise area on that original nail.
4. Blue preview shows the selected area only; the design is not painted blue.
5. Pick one of the 40 replacement colours. The original shading is retained.
6. Use Compare original to check against the source design.

Only clicked area protects disconnected regions, even if they have the same
colour. Select All matching areas if you intentionally want all matching parts.
Exact colour range starts at 6: lower for a tighter match; increase gradually
if you need more of a softly shaded area. Automatic photo grouping is an
estimate; use exact selection to control nearby brown, beige or cream shades.
Original design colours and Reset all pattern colours restore source artwork.
