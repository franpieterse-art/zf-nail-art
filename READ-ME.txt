ZF Nail Art V14 - Numbered design colours

Replace index.html, app.js and sw.js in your GitHub repository root, commit, wait for Render deployment, then refresh with Ctrl+Shift+R.

Choose a design. Its detected colours are numbered on the editing nail and listed with matching swatches beside it. Click a numbered swatch to select the corresponding Change 1st/2nd/3rd colour control. Numbers stay consistent when replacements are selected. Numbers are interface labels; they are not painted onto the hand, saved artwork or exported PNG. Natural/custom-painted nails require Detect colours on this nail to create the numbered key.

Includes V13 fill fix and V12 larger hand. This ZIP does not automatically update your Render deployment.

App and fill regression checks passed. Visual browser verification unavailable.
