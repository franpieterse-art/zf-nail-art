ZF Nail Art V15 - original V14 plus numbered colour blocks.

Select a design, choose Block 1 (main colour) or another numbered block, then choose a replacement colour. Numbered markers appear on the editor nail only and do not appear in exported designs.

Original V14 images, patterns, hand, tools, and layout retained. Colour areas are detected from image pixels, so photographic highlights and very similar colours may not separate perfectly.

Deploy all files together in the GitHub repository root and hard-refresh after deployment. This ZIP does not automatically deploy.
